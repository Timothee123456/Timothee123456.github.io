Your calculator's space for apps
  1. Add the Nwagra bookmark from nwagyu's guide
  2. Update your calculator twice on my.numworks.com
  3. On the installer page, click the bookmark, then drop NumPlay.nwa

If your calculator then says exam mode is on, Nwagra doesn't work with its version.

Installing NumPlay

  1. Plug your calculator into this computer with its USB cable
  2. Click Connect and choose your NumWorks in the list
  3. It installs by itself. Keep it plugged in

This replaces the other extra apps on your calculator.
